**Christians in Politics — Privacy Policy**

Effective date: **[date of publication]**

Christians in Politics Australia Ltd (ABN 93 697 747 630) ("CiP", "we", "us")
is an Australian registered charity that operates the Christians in Politics
member network, including the CiP Network mobile app and
network.christiansinpolitics.com (together, the "Platform").

We are a privacy-first organisation. We do not run advertising, we do not use
third-party analytics or tracking, and we never sell or rent your personal
information. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you
have. We handle personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988
(Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

## 1. What we collect

**Account and profile information (provided by you).** Your name, email
address, and password (stored only as a secure hash); optionally your mobile
number, job title, employer, a short bio, your suburb or postcode (used to
derive your state and electorates), a profile photo, and a church you attend.

**Sensitive information (provided by you, with your consent).** Because CiP is
a faith-based network, we ask for information the Privacy Act classifies as
sensitive: your Christian tradition or denomination, your affirmation of the
Nicene Creed, and — optionally — your political party affiliation. We collect
this only with your consent, given when you provide it. Your tradition, church
and party affiliation are private by default: party affiliation is shown to
other members only if you switch on "Show political affiliation on my public
profile".

**Content you create.** Posts, comments, reactions, polls and poll votes,
uploaded images and documents, messages to other members, event registrations,
group and organisation memberships, connection requests, and support requests
you send to the CiP team.

**Technical information.** Your account identifier, sign-in timestamps, and —
only if you turn on push notifications — a device push token. We do not
collect GPS location, contacts, advertising identifiers, or browsing history,
and the Platform contains no advertising or third-party analytics software.

**Age.** Membership requires you to confirm you are 16 or older. We record
that confirmation and when it was made. The Platform is not directed at
children under 16.

## 2. Why we collect it

We collect and use personal information to:

* operate your membership: your account, profile, feed, groups, events and
  messaging;
* connect you with other members, which is the purpose of the network — you
  control how discoverable you are (section 4);
* provide personal support: support requests are visible only to the CiP team,
  who use them to respond, pray, mentor, or make introductions you have agreed
  to;
* send you the notifications you have chosen: in-app alerts, emails, and
  optional push notifications;
* keep the community safe: reported or flagged content is reviewed by CiP
  moderators, assisted by automated (AI) monitoring of posts and comments for
  conduct that may breach the Member Conduct Agreement; and
* meet our legal obligations as an Australian charity.

We do not use your information for advertising or profiling, and we do not use
it to train AI models.

## 3. Who can see what

* **Other members** can see the profile fields you have chosen to share. In
  your Privacy settings you can hide individual fields (surname, photo, job
  title and employer, bio, state, electorate) or opt out of the member
  directory entirely. Tradition and church are never shown in the member
  directory. Party affiliation appears only if you opt in.
* **Members you block** cannot message you or send you connection requests,
  and you will not see their content.
* **The CiP team** (staff and authorised moderators) can access member records
  and support requests to run the Platform, respond to you, and moderate
  reported content. Group and organisation page owners see reports about
  content posted in their group or page.
* **The public** sees nothing. The member area is closed; no profile or post
  is visible without a member sign-in.

## 4. Disclosure to third parties

We disclose personal information only to service providers who process it on
our instructions so the Platform can function:

* **Supabase** — secure database, authentication and file storage. Data is
  hosted in Australia (AWS Sydney region).
* **Resend** — sends our emails (verification, notification and welcome
  emails).
* **Apple and Google** — deliver push notifications to your device, if you
  enable them; app-store transaction handling for app distribution.
* **Vercel** — hosts the web version of the Platform.

Some of these providers are located overseas (principally the United States).
Where personal information is processed overseas we take reasonable steps,
consistent with APP 8, to ensure it is protected to a comparable standard.

We may also disclose information where required or authorised by law, or where
you have expressly consented — for example, a facilitated introduction where
you have agreed to share specific details.

**We never sell, rent, or trade personal information, and we do not share it
with advertisers or data brokers.**

## 5. Security

All data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Access to member data is
restricted by row-level security controls so members see only what they are
entitled to see, and sensitive fields (tradition, church, party) are stored in
a separately protected table. Passwords are hashed, and you can add two-factor
authentication in Settings. Administrative access is limited to authorised CiP
personnel. No system is perfectly secure; if a data breach occurs that is
likely to result in serious harm we will notify affected members and the
Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) as required by the
Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

## 6. Retention and deletion

We keep personal information while your membership is active. You can delete
your account at any time in **Settings → Danger Zone → Delete account**; this
permanently removes your account and associated data from the Platform.
Support-request records and moderation records may be retained where we are
legally required to keep them or need them to resolve a dispute. Backup copies
are purged on a rolling basis.

## 7. Your choices and rights

You can, at any time and within the app:

* edit your profile, including removing any optional field;
* control field-level visibility, discoverability, and whether other members
  may message you or send connection requests;
* block or unblock any member;
* turn email and push notifications on or off, per type;
* delete your account entirely.

You may also ask us for access to, or correction of, the personal information
we hold about you, and we will respond within a reasonable period. Contact us
using the details below. If you are dissatisfied with our response you may
complain to the OAIC (oaic.gov.au).

## 8. Cookies and website

The Platform uses only the cookies and local storage necessary to keep you
signed in and remember your preferences. There are no advertising or
third-party analytics cookies.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be notified
in the app and by email, and the current version will always be available at
**[https://christiansinpolitics.com/privacy]**.
